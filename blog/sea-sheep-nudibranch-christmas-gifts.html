Frequently Asked Questions

What is a sea sheep nudibranch and why are they popular gifts?

The sea sheep nudibranch is a species of tiny, leaf-eating sea slug known scientifically as Costasiella kuroshimae. They feature distinctive rhinophores that look like sheep ears and black eyes that resemble cartoon illustrations. Their charming, harmless appearance has made them a viral sensation among animal lovers and marine biology fans.
