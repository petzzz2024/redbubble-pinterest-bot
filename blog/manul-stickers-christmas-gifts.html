How to Apply and Care for Your Vinyl Stickers

To make sure your holiday presents stay looking sharp through the new year and beyond, a little bit of application knowledge goes a long way. Whether you are gifting these decals or keeping them for yourself, follow these quick tips for a bubble-free finish:

  1. Clean the surface: Wipe down your water bottle, laptop, or notebook with a bit of rubbing alcohol to remove dust and oils. Let it dry completely.
  2. Peel slowly: Bend the backing paper slightly to lift the corner of the sticker without creasing the vinyl.
  3. Apply from the center: Stick down one edge and smooth it across with a credit card or your thumb to push air bubbles out toward the margins.
  4. Wash by hand: If the sticker is on a travel mug or water bottle, hand-washing will keep the colors vibrant and prevent peeling caused by harsh dishwasher cycles.
