Finding the Right Fit in Cozy Animal Hoodies for Winter

Buying clothing online can feel like a gamble, but scoring the right fit in meerkat office gift exchange ideas and everyday wear comes down to a few simple rules of thumb. Since these hoodies are designed for maximum lounging and boundary-setting, sizing up is almost always the right move.

Here are a few quick tips for keeping your winter wear looking fresh and feeling soft:

  1. Size up for slouch: If the recipient loves that oversized, blanket-like feel for winter hibernation, order one size larger than usual.
  2. Wash inside out: Protect the printed graphics by turning the garment inside out before tossing it into a cold machine wash.
  3. Skip the high heat: Air dry or tumble dry on low to keep the fleece lining cloud-soft and prevent shrinking.
  4. Gift with confidence: When in doubt, a roomy medium or large fits most adults comfortably as a cozy layering piece.
