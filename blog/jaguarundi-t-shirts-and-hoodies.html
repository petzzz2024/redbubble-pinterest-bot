Fit, Sizing, and Fabric Considerations

A great graphic print loses its appeal if the garment itself does not fit comfortably. Before hitting the checkout button, consider how you intend to wear your new clothes.

  • T-Shirts: Standard crewneck tees usually feature a regular retail fit. If you prefer an oversized, relaxed drape for lounging at home, sizing up is a smart move.
  • Hoodies: Fleece pullovers and zip-ups offer warmth and structure. Keep in mind that cotton-poly blends tend to shrink minimally on their own, but pre-shrunk options help maintain the intended fit over time.
  • Fabric Weight: Lightweight cotton works well for layering and warm weather, while heavyweight fabrics provide cozy durability for cooler autumn and winter months.

How to Care for Your Printed Apparel

Investing in custom-printed garments means you want to protect both the fabric and the direct-to-garment or screen-printed artwork. Simple laundry habits can drastically extend the lifespan of your favorite pieces.

  1. Turn Inside Out: Always flip your t-shirts and hoodies inside out before tossing them into the machine. This protects the graphic fibers from friction against other clothing and zippers.
  2. Wash in Cold Water: Hot water breaks down cotton fibers faster and can cause graphic inks to crack or fade prematurely. Cold cycles are gentle and energy-efficient.
  3. Skip the Harsh Bleach: Stick to mild, color-safe detergents and avoid chlorine bleach entirely, which degrades both the fabric dye and the print.
  4. Air Dry or Low Heat: High heat from a standard clothes dryer is the enemy of soft cotton blends and printed graphics. Hang drying on a rack is ideal, but if you must use a machine, select a low-heat or delicate tumble setting.

Treating your animal lover graphic sweatshirt and tees with a little extra care ensures the vibrant colors and sharp details remain intact wash after wash.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a jaguarundi?

A jaguarundi is a small wild cat native to Central and South America, known for its slender body, long tail, uniform coat, and rounded ears. Unlike spotted cats like jaguars, their fur is typically solid brown, gray, or reddish, giving them an appearance reminiscent of an otter or a weasel.

Are jaguarundi t-shirts and hoodies available in unisex sizing?

Yes, most independent print-on-demand apparel suppliers offer standard unisex sizing for t-shirts, crewnecks, and hoodies. This relaxed cut accommodates a wide range of body types comfortably, though consulting individual size charts before ordering is always recommended.

How do I prevent graphic prints from cracking on hoodies?

To prevent graphic prints from cracking, wash your hoodies inside out in cold water using a gentle cycle, and avoid using high heat in the tumble dryer. Air drying on a drying rack is the single best way to protect printed artwork over the long term.
