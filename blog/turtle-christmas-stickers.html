Pro Tips for Applying and Styling Your Vinyl Stickers

Getting a great sticker is only half the battle; putting it on your gear properly ensures it survives the elements. Whether you are decorating a thermos, a planner, or a tablet case, a little prep work goes a long way. Here is how to keep your favorite designs looking crisp all year round:

  • Clean the surface: Wipe down your water bottle or laptop with a little rubbing alcohol to remove dust and oils before application.
  • Start from the center: Lay down the middle first and use a credit card or your thumb to smooth outward, banishing air bubbles instantly.
  • Mind the wash cycle: If your sticker goes on a travel mug or water vessel, hand washing keeps the edges sealed much longer than a high-heat dishwasher cycle.
