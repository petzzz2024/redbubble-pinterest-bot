Choosing The Right Fit And Keeping Your Hoodie Soft

Getting the sizing right when buying presents can cause unnecessary headaches, but a few simple guidelines make gift shopping much easier. Here is a quick breakdown to help you pick the best fit:

Fit Type Best For Style Notes
Standard Fit Everyday wear and running errands True to size with a classic, clean silhouette
Oversized Maximum cocooning and hiding from people Size up one or two levels for extra slouchy comfort
Zip-Up Layering and indoor-outdoor transitions Easy to throw on and take off without messing up hair

To keep your garments looking fresh through countless winter washes, turn them inside out before throwing them in the machine. Wash on cold with gentle detergent and tumble dry on low heat or hang them up to air dry. This protects the printed design from cracking and keeps the inside fleece feeling like a comforting cloud against your skin.
