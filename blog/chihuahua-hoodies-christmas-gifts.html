Fit, Sizing, and Gifting Like a Pro

Ordering apparel online as a holiday present can feel like a guessing game. When you are picking out the right size for a friend, family member, or yourself, a few simple guidelines make all the difference.

How to Choose the Right Fit

  • Go oversized: When in doubt, size up. Hoodies are meant to feel like a wearable hug, especially during the gloomy depths of January.
  • Check the material blend: Look for a sturdy cotton-poly mix that holds its shape after multiple wash cycles and resists shrinking in the dryer.
  • Consider layering: If the recipient lives somewhere with brutal winters, an extra roominess ensures they can wear a thermal layer underneath without feeling stuffed like a holiday turkey.

Print Care and Keeping Your Gear Fresh

You want your winter wardrobe investment to last long past the holiday season. Proper maintenance ensures the graphics stay crisp and the fabric stays ridiculously soft.

  1. Turn the garment inside out before tossing it into the washing machine.
  2. Wash on a gentle, cold-water cycle to protect both the fabric and the print integrity.
  3. Skip the high heat in the dryer; hang it to dry or tumble on low to prevent premature wear.
