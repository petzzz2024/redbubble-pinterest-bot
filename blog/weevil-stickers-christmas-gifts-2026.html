Frequently Asked Questions

Why are weevil stickers so popular for office gifts?

Weevil stickers have become popular because they tap into a specific, lighthearted internet subculture that celebrates "snoots and boots." They serve as a perfect low-cost, high-personality gift for colleagues because they are funny and unexpected without being inappropriate. They provide a quick, humorous morale boost during the long days of the holiday season.

How can I make sure my stickers stay on my laptop or water bottle?

To ensure your stickers stay put, always apply them to a clean, dry, and flat surface. Wipe the area down with a small amount of isopropyl alcohol to remove any lingering grease or dust before sticking the design down. Use a flat, firm tool to press out any air bubbles, ensuring the edges are completely sealed against the surface.

Are these designs suitable for Secret Santa swaps?

Yes, these designs are excellent for Secret Santa swaps because they are gender-neutral and appeal to anyone with a sense of humor. They are particularly great for office settings because they aren't too personal, yet they show that you put thought into finding something unique rather than just grabbing a generic item from a local store.

Can I put these stickers on my car?

While these stickers are durable, they are primarily designed for indoor items like laptops, notebooks, and water bottles. If you choose to put them on your car, keep in mind that extreme weather, car washes, and direct sunlight may cause the colors to fade or the adhesive to weaken over time. For the best longevity, stick to indoor surfaces.
