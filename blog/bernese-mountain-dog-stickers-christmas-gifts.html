Pro Tips for Applying and Styling Vinyl Stickers

Getting your new holiday decals to look sharp and stay put for the long haul is mostly about surface prep. Before you peel the backing off any sticker, wipe down your laptop, water bottle, or clipboard with a little rubbing alcohol to remove any oily residue or dust. Press from the center outward using a smooth card or your thumb to chase away any trapped air bubbles.

If you are putting these stickers on items that go into the dishwasher, stick to hand washing to keep the colors vibrant through months of daily use. For gift wrapping, try sliding a few stickers inside a holiday greeting card as a fun bonus surprise before you mail it out to your favorite dog-obsessed relative.
